Last Swing

Support This Game

Grapple, swing, and launch through deadly obstacle courses! Time your hooks, dodge spikes, and race against the clock to escape!

Hold to attach your grapple hook to anchor points, swing to build momentum, and release at the perfect moment to launch yourself forward. Complete distance missions before time runs out, dodge deadly spikes, and bounce off pads to keep your run alive!

Features:

  • Satisfying grapple-hook physics with rope swinging mechanics
  • 2 beautiful parallax map themes: Blue Dusk & Toy Sky Park
  • Stage-based mission system with increasing difficulty
  • Bounce pads, spikes, and procedural level generation
  • Synthwave BGM and procedural sound effects
  • Stickman character with parkour animations
  • Global leaderboard with country flags

How to Play:

  • HOLD (click/tap/space) to attach grapple to nearest anchor
  • SWING to build momentum
  • RELEASE to launch forward
  • Complete each stage's distance target before time runs out!
